# VISITOR: THE NORMALCY — FEEDLINES

ビジター・ザ・ノーマルシー — Pretend Soft, Sharp X68030, 1995. Six disks. Requires 4MB, mouse recommended.

Vampire: The Masquerade – Bloodlines, nine years early, with Carl. Built on the DEUS EX NEVADA engine (dx/), forked into vn/.

**The fusion.** In Bloodlines the Masquerade is the law that vampires must stay hidden from humans. Carl is an alien who wants everyone to agree he is a normal human. He has been doing the Masquerade alone, badly, out loud, his whole life. In this game it turns out everybody else has been doing it too, and much better. Los Angeles is full of visitors pretending to be people, and they all feed on people somehow. The law that keeps them hidden is **THE NORMALCY**. Its First Rule is *Be Normal*. Carl breaks it nightly, on stream, to forty viewers, and his only defense is the truest thing he has ever said: *nobody believes anything on a stream.*

Everything marked *(new)* is new lore for this cart. Nothing here settles Carl's real name, his species, his parents, his homeworld, or the facility. The Consulate cannot classify him either. His intake form says **SPECIES: PROVISIONAL**, and the species the player picks is the one the clerk guessed.

---

## 1. Look and feel

- **Engine:** the DX engine forked: 68k-X 512x256 tall pixels, 12-bit color, FM sound, top-down 3/4 tiles, per-pixel light and line of sight, stealth AI, Carl's live arm poses.
- **Night only.** Bloodlines is always night. So is this. LA is sodium orange and neon pink; interiors are lamp pools in black.
- **True forms.** Visitors look human in public. Inside their own places (the Crash Pad, the Consulate, the drains), or when Carl has a sight gift running, the game draws them as what they are: grey big-headed probers, pale tall Nordics, lizard-faced executives, little green Martians, mothmen.
- **Portraits:** 90s anime cels through the Codex → 16-color pipeline, reused from DX where the character is the same person.
- **The chat stays in the HUD.** *(new)* It is the reason Carl is alive: the Consulate ruled that a stream is the safest place an alien can show his face.

## 2. Systems (what Bloodlines has)

| Bloodlines | Here | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Blood pool | **VITALITY** | What visitors take from humans. Fuels gifts, heals (MEND). Drains slowly. Shown as a bar in the HUD. |
| Feeding | **FEED** (hold F) | On a human who is unaware, asleep, knocked out or charmed. Each species feeds differently (below) and the screen shows it. Let go when the bar is full. Hold on past that and the human dies: **Humanity -1**. |
| Masquerade | **NORMALCY** | Five strikes. Being seen feeding, using a visible gift in front of humans, a Cryptid being seen up close. At five, the Men in White Suits come for you (game over). Some quests and the Greys' MISSING TIME take strikes back. |
| Humanity | **HUMANITY** | 0-10. Killing innocents and cruel choices cost it. Low Humanity means more FREAKOUTS and colder dialogue. At 0 Carl stops believing he's human, and the game ends. For him that is the worst possible ending. |
| Frenzy | **FREAKOUT** | Carl's anxiety, finally given a mechanic. Vitality near zero or a big hit at low Humanity: the screen tunnels, Carl lunges at the nearest human for a few seconds. Feeding ends it. |
| Disciplines | **GIFTS** | Three per species, levels 1-5, bought with XP. F1-F3 (and the mouse wheel on the gift bar). Plus **MEND** (Q) for everyone. |
| Clans | **SPECIES** | Seven, chosen at intake (questionnaire or pick). Changes gifts, feeding, some dialogue, some quests. |
| Persuasion / Intimidation / Seduction | **DENIAL / RANT / CHARM** | Social skills that gate dialogue. DENIAL is DX's: insist so hard on being human that people give up. |
| XP from quests | XP | Only from quests and smart solutions (as in Bloodlines). Spend it on skills and gifts on the CHARACTER screen. |
| Havens | Havens | An apartment per district: bed (save), computer (mail), stash, radio (DEEP NIGHT WITH DEB). |
| Weapons | Weapons | Tire iron, fire axe, katana, sledge, .38, Glock, shotgun, SMG, rifle, crossbow, flamethrower. |
| Malkavian dialogue | **THE BROADCAST** | Broadcast Carl gets different lines in key conversations, and the chat sometimes prints prophecies from the signal. |

Everything else comes from the DX engine: stealth, light, lockpicks, hacking, keypads, cameras, carry bodies, inventory grid, conversations with skill-gated choices.

Carl can be any of them. The clerk guessed, and none of the seven will confirm he's theirs. *(new)* This is the cart's version of his parents question: everyone has a theory and nobody has an answer.

## 4. Factions *(new)*

- **THE CONSULATE** (Camarilla) keeps the Normalcy. Its seat is the Pretend Co. Tower Downtown. Its Commissioner is **CEO LINDA**, the only seat-holder nobody can classify either. Pretend Co. sells Normalcy as a subscription: disguises, papers, cleanup. The Men in White Suits are the subcontractor who cleans up.
- **THE CRASHERS** (Anarchs) crash-landed, never registered, and don't pay. Their bar is **THE CRASH PAD**. They're led by **ROSCOE NINE-PLANETS** *(new)*, a Martian greaser still furious about Pluto. **PILOT X** drinks there and nobody knows what he is.
- **THE INVADERS** (Sabbat) want to drop the act and take the planet. Their prophet is **DYSLEXIO**: Old Face, deported in the prologue, back in a Magneto helmet. He gives the sincere monologue about being made to read the world in someone else's order. No misspelling gags.
- **THE YOKOIDS** (Kuei-jin) aren't aliens at all: they're software. They hide too. **EMPRESS YOKO** rules Little Tokyo from the top of a pachinko tower and has no interest in anyone's pod.
- **THE MEN IN WHITE SUITS** (the Hunters / the Giovanni's role in the heist). Humans who know. **MR. WHITE**'s mansion in the hills is where the pod ends up.
- **PILLHEADS** (ghouls). Humans who take Prop Pills see through the Normalcy. The pills were recalled for being more effective than placebo, which turns out to mean *they let you see who's really at the party*. Visitors pay pillheads in more pills.

## 5. The pod

**THE NEVADA POD** *(new)* (the Ankaran Sarcophagus) is a sealed lifepod pulled out of the desert "above and below Nevada". Everyone says the First Visitor is inside, and that opening it ends the Normalcy (Gehenna). It came into port on the freighter *Lady Nevada* with the crew drained, went to the museum, got stolen, turned up in the White mansion, and finished in Linda's penthouse.

Carl hopes, quietly and then not quietly, that it's something to do with his parents. The game never says. JB Garfield traces the shipping manifest. It says **CONTENTS: 1 (ONE). STATUS: STILL IN TRANSIT.** By the time anyone opens the pod, Pilot X has swapped the contents for explosives. Asked what was in it originally, he says: *spoilers.*

## 7. Map (disks)

- **Disk A — PROLOGUE.** Intake (questionnaire, species), THE HEARING at the Planetarium, Pilot X's tutorial in the Pretend Co. shipping yard.
- **Disk B — SANTA MONICA.** Streets and pier; Carl's haven; Tommy's apartment; the Lost & Found club (the Lindas); the free clinic; the Ocean House; the Invader beach house (main).
- **Disk C — DOWNTOWN.** Streets; the Crash Pad; the Pretend Co. Tower (lobby, penthouse); the *Lady Nevada* freighter; the museum; the storm drains (Gus); the Greys' Clinic; skyline haven.
- **Disk D — HOLLYWOOD.** The Boulevard; the Velvet Spook; the Luckee Star motel (JB); the Hollywood cemetery (the returnees); the White mansion (party heist).
- **Disk E — LITTLE TOKYO.** Streets; Empress Yoko's pachinko tower; the fish market (the frame-up of Roscoe).
- **Disk F — THE END.** The Planetarium under the Invaders (Dyslexio, the Flatwoods); the Pretend Co. Tower assault; the pod. Five endings: Consulate, Crashers, Yokoids, Carl alone, Invaders.
