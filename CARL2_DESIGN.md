# CARL 2: HU-MAN BOOGALOO — design

The last game for the SUPER-16. It came out after the generation ended.

## The pitch

YOKO's true ending closed the SUPER-16 generation: "SEE YOU NEXT GENERATION?" CARL 2 is the answer nobody asked for. It's the late-life cash-in sequel: the one with the shark, the extreme redesign, the clip show, the crossover episode and the trip to space. It is a big game in the "dream project" mold (Barkley 2 scope, genres piled on genres), and it's actually finished.

It's everything Carl: the parking lot, the A.S.S., Prop Pills deliveries, the Mall of the Future, bongs being thrown at Lindas, arguing as combat, chat as company, smoking to see what the sun doesn't show, the tapes, Desert Bus, Garf's diner, the facility below Nevada, and the question under all of it: where are his parents, and what is his name.

## The meta story

The three late Gen-2 games are one argument about what a character is:

| Game | Stance | What the saves mean |
|---|---|---|
| FACE | control: what could happen | unfinished games are potential |
| YOKO | support: what happened | every save is the record |
| CARL 2 | identity: what happens when it keeps going | every save is **continuity**, and the sequel keeps trying to rewrite it |

In CARL 1 the White Suits contained Carl. In CARL 2 they've rebranded: the **Head of Containment** is now the **HEAD OF CONTENT**, and containment is franchise management. The **Sequel Committee** (Head of Content, the FOCUS GROUP, and LEGAL) is making Carl marketable. Their product is **HU-MAN**, a focus-tested "normal human" Carl. It's literally what Carl always claims to be, and he is exactly the Carl the character bible warns against: composed, square-jawed, a quip machine. **Every line HU-MAN says is one of the bible's "Too generic" examples.**

The fight is whether Carl stays Carl while the series jumps the shark around him.

## Systems

### The action core (most of the game)
Top-down action RPG, 16×16 tiles, scrolling maps connected by exits.

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows | move (8 directions) |
| A | throw your equipped **bong** (hold to keep facing) |
| B | talk / use; otherwise a dodge roll |
| C | **SMOKE** (haze): reveals hidden things, slows enemies. Hold C when the HU-MAN meter is full to transform |
| START | menu: BONGS, TAPES, STATUS, ASK CHAT, SAVE, TITLE |

- **COMPOSURE** is health. Losing it isn't a game over: "CARL LOST HIS COOL", and you're back at the map entrance, a little poorer.
- **PROP BUX** drop from enemies. Spend them at the Bong Emporium.
- **Pretzels** heal.
- **The objective marker** at the top of the screen is a Committee note ("KIDS GET LOST"). It points at the next thing to do, and the test bot follows it too.
- **Chat** runs as a ticker along the side. Some of its messages are hints; some come from `user_000`.

### Bongs (the loot)
Every bong is generated: size × material × up to two mods, plus a name.
- **Sizes**: MINI (fast), REGULAR, BIG (slow, heavy), FIVE-FOOT (taller than him, pierces).
- **Materials**:

  | Material | Effect |
  |---|---|
  | GLASS | balanced |
  | CERAMIC | heavy |
  | NEON | fast |
  | PRETZEL | heals on hit |
  | HAUNTED | passes through walls |
  | LICENSED | homes in on Lindas and Committee staff |
  | LEGACY | four-shade green; bonus damage to sequel stuff |
  | GOLD | rare |

- **Mods**: BOOMERANG, SPLIT, BOUNCY, HOMING, PIERCE, SMOKY (leaves haze), SHATTER (area damage), ARGUMENTATIVE (the enemies it hits argue with each other, which stuns them), EXTREME (+50% damage, +HUMAN% while equipped).
- **Names** follow the pattern "FIVE-FOOT HAUNTED BONG OF MILD GRIEVANCE".
- **Rarity**: COMMON, UNCOMMON, RARE, EXTREME, LEGACY.
- **THE BIG ONE** is unique. It's the original from the Emporium tape: "There's only ONE big one. We keep replacing it."
- **Inventory** holds 8. At the Bong Emporium you can SELL, and the BONG-O-MATIC combines two bongs into one.

### HUMAN% (the identity meter)
- **HU-MAN power**: the Committee gives Carl the POWER BONG. Hitting enemies fills a meter; hold C at full to go HU-MAN ("BY THE POWER OF NEVADA!").
- **In HU-MAN form** Carl is big, invulnerable and deals triple damage for 10 seconds.
- **Every transformation adds HUMAN%.** So do accepting Committee NOTES (optional power-ups) and using HU-MAN QUIPs in arguments.
- **Tapes, sincere choices, and letting chat talk** bring it down.
- **At 40%** Carl's portrait gets sunglasses. **At 70%** some of his lines swap to generic HU-MAN lines, and the ending changes.
- **Status screen**: "SPECIES: HUMAN(?) 23%", a callback to CARL 1's "HUMAN(?)".

### Arguments (turn-based, upgraded from CARL 1)
- **The basics:** LOGIC, GRIEVANCE, DENIAL and RANT against PATIENCE, plus DEFLECT, ASK CHAT, SMOKE and ITEM.
- **HU-MAN QUIP (new):** big damage and +HUMAN%. Against HU-MAN himself it does nothing, because he's immune to his own lines.
- **COMPANION (new):**
  - Garfield: EVIDENCE, which exposes the foe's weakness.
  - The Shark: CHOMP.
- **Big scene:** foes are drawn large in color, with portraits.

### Set pieces (pseudo-3D, one shared projector)
- **DESERT BUS TURBO**: an Out-Run-style bus race to Garf's diner against HU-MAN's convertible. There are 8 "hours" of checkpoints.
- **JUMP THE SHARK**: water-skiing on Lake Mead behind the A.S.S. (which LEGAL has reclassified as a boat). You slalom the buoys for RATINGS, then the ramp. Hold A for speed, release on the ramp, then pick a trick in the air.
- **IN SPACE**: Space-Harrier style over a checkerboard. Carl rides the A.S.S. and throws bongs into the screen.

### THE BOOGALOO (dance-off)
Call and response. HU-MAN performs a four-beat phrase (↑ ↓ ← → A); Carl repeats it on the beat. A tug-of-war RATINGS bar decides it. The music is FM funk.

## Episodes

Each episode opens on a sitcom title card and the Committee's NOTE. Between episodes, Dispatch's radio in the A.S.S. starts the next one, and you can travel back to places you've visited.

0. **COLD OPEN — PREVIOUSLY ON CARL.** CARL 1's ending, in four-shade green: the voice, and "below below". Then the sequel logo slams in, all chrome and lens flare, and Carl wakes up in color. "Why am I a different color. Chat. Is this a filter?"
1. **PILOT.** The lot, the A.S.S. and the Mall of the Future.
   - Dispatch has one delivery, "TO: BELOW BELOW", from a sender written in `[[[[[` glyphs, and the mall's new SEQUEL CENTER is holding it.
   - You get past Linda's ban, meet the Committee, and receive HU-MAN power.
   - Boss: ROBO MALL COP. Then an argument with CEO LINDA over his likeness: "Humans can be licensed."
2. **DESERT BUS TURBO.**
   - The race, then Garf's diner.
   - A case: find three clues and ACCUSE the waitress, who turns out to be LINDA LITE.
   - Garfield joins, and there's a desert action area.
   - Boss: THE CONTENT VAN.
   - Garfield reads the label: it bounced off Lake Mead.
3. **JUMP THE SHARK.**
   - The ski run, then the jump. "THE SERIES HAS JUMPED THE SHARK."
   - The shark, BRUCE, joins. He's a veteran: "I've been jumped by better."
   - HU-MAN debuts in a skirmish you can't finish.
   - After this the world goes EXTREME: neon, lens flares, more of everything.
4. **THE CLIP SHOW / THE CROSSOVER.**
   - Spooky Ghost hosts a clip show of CARL 1 in four shades, padded with clips that never happened. The Committee "remembers" Carl as a cop and as a mayor; Carl: "That was a PROPOSAL."
   - Then the PRETEND CO. backlot, with three soundstages to visit:

     | Soundstage | Who | What happens |
     |---|---|---|
     | FACE's stream set | New Face | "You're not the one who got me out." "I'd like to be." |
     | PEE KID³'s school | Pee Kid | He reads the label and asks if it's Carl's name. |
     | YOKO LTD. | Yoko | Asked to translate it, she refuses: "It's a name. Names aren't mine to translate." |

   - Boss: THE CROSSOVER, a stitched-together monster.
5. **IN SPACE.**
   - The Harrier run.
   - The RED MOON, and COMRADE CARL, the one whose family never crashed. LEGAL stamps him NON-CANON. You argue with yourself.
   - His line: "I had them the whole time. It's not as good as you think."
   - Boss: THE SYNDICATOR.
6. **A VERY SPECIAL EPISODE.**
   - No laugh track.
   - The facility, then below below: the Lost+Found's back room, a warehouse of everything every game ever lost, with haze puzzles.
   - Boss: the HEAD OF CONTENT.
   - The counter: "You just missed them. Again." If you're eligible, they left a claim ticket with his name on it.
7. **SERIES FINALE — THE BOOGALOO.** HU-MAN in three phases: action, argument, dance. Then the Committee's final offer.

| Ending | How | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| RENEWED | HUMAN% ≥ 70, or sign the Season 3 contract | Carl becomes HU-MAN. The credits tease "CARL 3: HU-MAN 3000". The title keeps HU-MAN (`carl2_meta.renewed`). |
| CANCELED | choose CANCEL IT | The studio lights go off: "CANCELED AFTER ONE SEASON." Carl and Bruce are back in the lot. |

## Content rules (from the bible)

- **The parents' fate stays open.** They are never seen, only just missed. Carl learns his name; the player doesn't.
- **Comrade Carl, cop Carl and mayor Carl are labeled.** They appear as proposals, fake clips or NON-CANON, never as biography.
- **Carl stays the little alien.** The muscled redesign is the villain. Carl's own lines keep the anxious, forming-in-the-moment voice; the polished ones belong to HU-MAN.
- **Weed is a verb, not his personality.** Smoking reveals things, and that's it.
- **Visual canon.** Carl is in color because marketing colorized him, and he's uneasy about it. The other Gen-1 cast stay in their four shades unless YOKO's true ending has been seen. Gen-2 natives are in color.
- **TP stays outside the lore.** TP never appears as a person. There's a TP cartridge in a bargain bin, and Carl doesn't know who that is.

## Saves (localStorage)

- `carl2_save`: episode, map, position, flags, bongs, stats, tapes and quests.
- `carl2_meta`: boots, endings seen, last ending, `renewed`, `dream`, best HUMAN%.
- It reads:
  - `carl_meta` (the recap, and "CONTINUED" if CARL 1's secret was seen);
  - `face_meta`, `pk_meta`, `yoko_meta` (the crossover; `yoko_meta.perma` makes the publisher YOKO LTD.; `yoko_meta.ends.true` puts the Gen-1 cast in color);
  - `tp_mem` (the bargain bin).
- TP's NEWER HARDWARE award lists CARL 2.
